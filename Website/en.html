What do I need?+

A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple Silicon or Intel, and an Apple TV or another compatible AirPlay receiver on the same local network. FFmpeg and FFprobe are included in the app. No separate installation is needed.

Will it work with any TV?+

You need an Apple TV or a TV with a compatible AirPlay receiver. Chromecast is not supported. Beamly plays your local files; it does not connect to streaming services or play DRM-protected content.

What about 4K and HDR?+

For compatible files, Beamly preserves the HEVC/H.264 video stream, including supported HDR10/HLG formats. 4K/HDR over AirPlay has not yet been tested on physical receivers; stability and compatibility depend on your device and network. Details and limitations ↗

What should I know before the first launch?+

The community build is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple, so macOS may show an unidentified developer warning. Follow the first-launch instructions. Allow local-network access if macOS asks. Use a trusted network and do not expose the media port to the internet.

Is it free?+

Yes. Beamly is an open-source project with no subscription. The app’s own code is available under the MIT license; bundled media tools and Sparkle have their own licenses. Source code and licenses ↗
