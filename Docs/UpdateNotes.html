4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

Audio and subtitle language preferences. In Settings → Track preferences, choose your preferred audio language or original audio, and a subtitle language, automatic selection, or off. These independent choices are saved across launches and applied when each video opens, including the next queue entry.

Original audio requires an original-track marker in the file. Missing preferred audio uses the file's default track; missing preferred subtitles stay off. Language selection matches embedded metadata and adjacent subtitle filenames, and prefers full captions over forced-only captions. The initial file-default and automatic settings retain previous behavior.

Manual track choices survive seeking and subtitle timing changes. This release also fixes the mapping of AVFoundation's generated forced-only subtitle views when playback switches to HLS. Preference controls and help are available in all five interface languages.

Supported 4K HEVC/H.264 video keeps its original quality and supported HDR10/HLG metadata. Caption corrections remain aligned after seeking and track changes. Your queue, timing corrections, and preferences are preserved. Original video and subtitle files are never changed.

FFmpeg and ffprobe remain included for Apple Silicon and Intel. No Homebrew media packages are required.

Dolby Vision remuxing is not supported. Bitmap subtitles require compatibility conversion; ASS styling is simplified. See the full release notes for supported profiles and verification limits.
