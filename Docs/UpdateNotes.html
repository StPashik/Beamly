4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

Chapters and seek previews. Choose an embedded chapter from the chapter icon beside the timeline or Playback → Chapters. The menu shows the current chapter and source titles, with numbered labels for unnamed chapters.

Hover over the timeline or drag its handle to inspect a small source frame, time, and chapter title. Hovering does not move playback or change your saved position. Dragging seeks when released and preserves paused or playing state. The main midpoint image stays static.

Preview time follows the native slider's thumb bounds, including the ends of long videos. Frame requests use cancellation, debounce, and a small per-video cache. Chapter controls and preview messages are available in all five interface languages.

Chapter seeks retain the original movie's clock through HLS buffer rebuilds. Native MP4/MOV chapter loading remains available if probing fails, including chapters without a language tag. This release also fixes the initialization filename for video-only HLS.

Supported 4K HEVC/H.264 video keeps its original quality and supported HDR10/HLG metadata. Caption corrections remain aligned after seeking and track changes. Your queue, timing corrections, and preferences are preserved. Original video and subtitle files are never changed.

FFmpeg and ffprobe remain included for Apple Silicon and Intel. No Homebrew media packages are required.

Dolby Vision remuxing is not supported. Bitmap subtitles require compatibility conversion; ASS styling is simplified. See the full release notes for supported profiles and verification limits.
