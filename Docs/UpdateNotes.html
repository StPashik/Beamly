4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

AirPlay reconnection and interface fixes. Keep the selected receiver while preparing a video or changing audio/subtitles. Retain the source position and request to continue playback across stream rebuilds and seeks. A genuine disconnection pauses playback.

An already selected route is prepared before requesting device selection. If it does not become active within three seconds, a manual Play request opens the native AirPlay picker. Playback remains blocked without an active external route; Pause and Stop cancel pending preparation. Physical receiver retesting of these fixes is still required; reconnection is not guaranteed on every device or network.

Volume controls have been removed throughout Beamly; use your TV, receiver, or its remote. The menu bar mini remote no longer includes Stop, while playback, skips, previous/next, and the sleep timer remain. Choose audio tracks and per-video timing in the audio menu; manual Stop remains in the Playback menu.

Watch history now matches Beamly's compact custom window controls, fixed width, translucent background, adaptive accent, and glass panels. Continue watching, watched videos, and history management remain available. Reduced transparency and earlier macOS material fallbacks are retained.

All five interface languages are complete. Your queue, history, resume positions, timing corrections, and preferences are preserved. Original videos and subtitle files are never changed. FFmpeg and ffprobe remain included for Apple Silicon and Intel.

Physical testing of audio timing correction was not performed. Dolby Vision remuxing is not supported. Bitmap subtitles require compatibility conversion; ASS styling is simplified.
