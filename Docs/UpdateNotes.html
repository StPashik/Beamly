Beamly 1.0.13 starts eligible H.264 and 4K HEVC MKV files as soon as the first HLS segments are ready. You can resume at a saved position and seek anywhere while the rest of the movie is prepared.

  • Keep HDR10 and HLG when copying compatible HEVC video, including MKV files that store decoder parameters in their keyframes.
  • Switch compatible Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus tracks on Apple TV while Beamly prepares the rest of the stream.
  • Reopen the AirPlay picker immediately after dismissing it without selecting a receiver.
  • Clear abandoned video buffers and report when the disk is too full to prepare a video.

Apple TV 4K testing confirmed 4K HLG and HDR10 playback. The HLG trial also confirmed audio and full-timeline seeking. Compatibility varies with the receiver and source.
