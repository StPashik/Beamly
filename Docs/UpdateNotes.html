4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

AirPlay reconnection and a cleaner interface. Keep the selected receiver and playback intent when changing audio or subtitles. An already selected route is prepared before opening device selection; playback still requires an active AirPlay receiver. Volume is controlled on the receiver, and the mini remote no longer includes Stop. Watch history now matches Beamly's compact translucent window and glass panels.

Watch history, audio timing, and menu bar remote. Open Watch history from the header's clock icon or the File menu to continue unfinished videos or restart watched ones. Keep up to 100 videos locally, including resume positions and timing corrections after queue removal. Existing positions migrate automatically; clearing history does not delete movies or change the active queue.

Choose Audio timing… in the audio menu to move sound earlier or later in 0.1-second steps, from −10 to +10 seconds. The correction is saved per video and survives seeking and track changes. Compatible video, resolution, and HDR metadata are preserved; AAC and supported surround copy paths remain available. Reset to zero to allow direct playback again. Audio outside the source's time range is silent. Physical testing of audio correction on an AirPlay receiver was not performed.

The Beamly icon in the macOS menu bar opens a mini remote for playback, skips, previous/next, and the sleep timer. Closing the main window now keeps Beamly and playback available. Choose Show Beamly to reopen it or Quit Beamly to exit. Play still requires an AirPlay receiver.

Choose Playback → Sleep Timer for 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, or 120 minutes. The timer continues across pauses, seeks, track changes, and queue transitions. On expiry, playback/preparation stops and the resume position is saved. Stop, clearing the queue, removing its last entry, or quitting cancels the timer; it is not restored on relaunch.

All new controls are available in five interface languages. Your queue, timing corrections, and preferences are preserved. Original videos and subtitle files are never changed. FFmpeg and ffprobe remain included for Apple Silicon and Intel.

Moved or renamed videos must be added from their new location. Dolby Vision remuxing is not supported. Bitmap subtitles require compatibility conversion; ASS styling is simplified.
