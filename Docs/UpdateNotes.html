4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

More convenient subtitles. Beamly finds matching SRT, ASS, SSA, and VTT files beside your video, including names such as Movie.ru.srt. An exact-basename file or the only match is selected automatically; choose between multiple language files in the subtitles menu.

Choose Subtitles → Subtitle timing… to move captions earlier or later in 0.5-second steps, or reset the correction. Positive values show subtitles later. Each movie keeps its correction with its saved queue entry, including after relaunch. Original video and subtitle files are never changed.

Supported 4K HEVC/H.264 video keeps its original quality and supported HDR10/HLG metadata. Caption corrections remain aligned after seeking and track changes. Your queue and preferences are preserved.

FFmpeg and ffprobe remain included for Apple Silicon and Intel. No Homebrew media packages are required.

Dolby Vision remuxing is not supported. Bitmap subtitles require compatibility conversion; ASS styling is simplified. See the full release notes for supported profiles and verification limits.
