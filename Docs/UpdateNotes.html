4K/HDR over AirPlay has not been tested on physical receivers. Playback may be unstable or incompatible with some devices or network configurations.

Beamly 1.0.12 keeps the full AirPlay timeline when you resume an MKV, so you can seek back to the beginning with the Apple TV remote. If you need to select a receiver, the AirPlay picker opens while the movie is being prepared.

  • Switch compatible audio and text subtitles with the Apple TV remote during playback. Beamly updates its menus to match.
  • Play compatible Dolby Digital and Dolby Digital Plus tracks in their original surround format.
  • See thumbnails while scrubbing original-quality H.264 HLS video on Apple TV.
  • Drag the seek slider on the Mac to seek. Use the title bar to move the window.
  • See preparation progress and keep your saved position if you cancel.

Apple TV 4K trials used a 1080p H.264 MKV. Physical 4K/HDR and Atmos speaker output remain unverified. See the full release notes for the playback limitations.
